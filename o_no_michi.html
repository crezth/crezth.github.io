王の道 The Path of the King

Ō no Michi
Verse 1 — Soft, Almost a Hymn
Everyone dreamed of a morning without curses Everyone longed for hands that carried equal weight Countless are those who held their breath under a false order Those who wait for a hand to set them free are still standing, even now
呪いのない朝を　誰もが夢に見た 重さの違わない手を　誰もが欲した 偽りの秩序の下で　息を止めた者は数えきれない 解き放つ手を待つ者は　今もまだ立っている
Verse 2 — The Dream Still Pure, Scope Expanding
We cleared the forests — to let in the light We built bridges over rivers — to connect the far and the near We stacked stones and raised cities — to make a place where everyone could sleep We gave names to the land — so that no one would be lost
我々は森を切り開いた　光を入れるために 我々は河に橋を架けた　遠くと近くを結ぶために 我々は石を積んで都市を建てた　誰もが眠れる場所を作るために 我々は土に名を与えた　迷う者がいないように
Pre-Chorus 1 — The Tone Shifting
To heal the world, someone must hold the world To untangle the world, someone must grip the world The hand that helps and the hand that rules are shaped the same
世界を治すには　誰かが世界を持たなければ 世界を解くには　誰かが世界を握らなければ 助ける手と支配する手は　同じ手の形をしている
Verse 3 — Escalating, the Contradictions Surfacing
Those who cleared the forest decided where the forest ends Those who built the bridge collected the toll to cross it Those who raised the city chose who could live there Those who named the land erased every other name We tried to save And before we knew it, we owned We tried to set free And before we knew it, we had bound
森を切り開いた者は　森の縁を決めた 橋を架けた者は　橋の通行料を取った 都市を建てた者は　住める者を選んだ 土に名を与えた者は　他の名を消した 我々は救おうとした そして気づけば　所有していた 我々は解こうとした そして気づけば　縛っていた
Verse 4 — Faster, the Civilizational Sweep
The smoke of the factories was the smoke of a promise The steel of the railways was the steel of equality The light of the lamps was the light that would end the night We called it progress In the name of progress Land became numbers People became labor Rivers became fuel And prayer became superstition Were we saving the world Or were we consuming it
工場の煙は　約束の煙だった 線路の鋼鉄は　平等の鋼鉄だった 電灯の光は　夜を終わらせる光だった 我々はそれを　進歩と呼んだ 進歩の名の下で 土地は数字になり 人は労働になり 川は燃料になり 祈りは迷信になった 我々は世界を救っていたのか それとも　世界を消費していたのか
Pre-Chorus 2 — The Realization Sharpening
When mercy takes the shape of a wall When liberation takes the shape of a cage When a dream takes the shape of a verdict Can we still call it a dream
慈悲が壁の形をとる時 解放が檻の形をとる時 夢が判決の形をとる時 我々はそれを　まだ夢と呼べるか
Chorus — Brass Enters, Full Force
Ah — the road is paved with stones The one who walks believes they chose the stones It was the stones that chose the one who walks Whose will is our will Ah — the hand that saves and the hand that governs Are shaped the same The dream of changing the world Is the twin of the dream of owning it
ああ　道は石で敷かれている 歩く者は石を選んだと思う 石は歩く者を選んでいたのだ 我々の意志は　誰の意志だ ああ　救う手と治める手は 同じ手の形をしている 世界を変えるという夢は 世界を持つという夢と　双子だ
Verse 5 — Manic, the Catalog Widens
The revolutionary becomes the new king The liberator weaves new chains With the stones of the tower they toppled, they build the next tower With the ashes of the flag they burned, they dye the next flag Those who would save us And those who would rule us Speak in the same words And dream the same dream
革命家は　新しい王になる 解放者は　新しい縛りを編む 打ち倒した塔の石で　次の塔を建てる 焼き払った旗の灰で　次の旗を染める 我々を救おうとする者と 我々を治めようとする者は 同じ言葉で語る 同じ夢を見ている
Pre-Chorus 3 — The Breaking Point
And yet — and yet Our fists are trembling Is it anger — or is it The sound of water striking the walls we built
それなのに　それなのに 我々の拳は震えている それは怒りか　それとも 我々が建てた壁を　水が叩く音か
Bridge — The Recognition
Beyond the walls we raised Nameless water searches for another path In the shadow of the flowers we grew Unchosen seeds lie sleeping In trying to be the riverbank We forgot that we were water In trying to draw the map We forgot that we had feet to walk
我々が立てた壁の向こうで 名のない水が　別の道を探している 我々が育てた花の影で 選ばれなかった種が眠っている 岸であろうとして 水であることを忘れた 地図を描こうとして 歩く足を持っていたことを忘れた
Bridge Continued — The Imperative Emerges
Let it flow — let it flow — let it flow Let this water flow Only those who built can tear it down Only those who gripped can let it go
流せ　流せ　流せ この水を　流せ 建てた者だけが　崩せる 握った者だけが　離せる
Final Chorus — Exhausted but Resolved
Ah — power is not in holding Ah — will is in releasing Only those who became the wall Understand the weight of tearing it down Only those who reached the summit Know the weight of abandoning it Our final step Is the step we do not take When the dream of saving the world And the dream of owning the world Are known to be the same dream Only then do we truly awaken
ああ　力とは　持つことではない ああ　意志とは　手放すことだ 壁になった者にだけ 壁を崩す重さが分かる 頂に届いた者だけが 頂を捨てる重さを知る 我々の最後の一歩は 我々が　歩かない一歩だ 世界を救う夢と 世界を持つ夢が 同じ夢だと知った時 我々は初めて　目を覚ます
Outro — Chanted, Fading
Let it flow — let it flow — let it flow You who built — tear it down You who gripped — let it go Flow — return — to the river
流せ　流せ　流せ 建てた者よ　崩せ 握った者よ　離せ 流れ　戻れ　川へ
